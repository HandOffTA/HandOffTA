**Recruiting and talent acquisition leader who builds practical AI tools for hiring teams.**

I've spent my career inside the hiring process: sourcing, screening, closing, and fixing the parts that slow teams down. Now I build the tools I wish I'd had. They're small, focused, and made for recruiters and hiring managers who need results this week, not next quarter.
